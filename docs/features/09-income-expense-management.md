# Income & Expense Management

> Linear: [LOG-105](https://linear.app/logiqdev/issue/LOG-105/income-and-expense-management) · Status: Backlog

> **Canonical for:** the agency ledger: every income and expense record, financial categories, VAT management and reporting, supplier invoice intake (AI/OCR), recurring expenses, payroll and employee cost records, payment and settlement tracking on ledger records, financial periods, profit and loss, project and customer profitability.
>
> **Boundary with 08 Finance:** 08 owns what the agency **bills and collects** (invoices, payments, subscriptions, payment providers). 09 owns the **ledger**. Every 08 invoice and payment automatically creates or updates an income record here; 09 never issues customer invoices. "Invoice upload" in this module means supplier invoices and external documents, not invoices generated by 08.
>
> **Depends on:** [01](./01-users-organizations-permissions.md) (LOG-406) for permissions, approval rights, separation of duties, branch-level finance and cost centers (01 §34) · [08 Finance](./08-financial-management-stripe-integration.md) (LOG-104) for invoices, payments and subscriptions · [02 CRM](./02-crm-sales-management.md) (LOG-404) for Customers and Vendors (02 §2a) · [10 Employees](./10-employee-management-system.md) (LOG-107) for compensation data · [18 Time Tracking](./18-time-tracking.md) (LOG-408) for employee cost allocation to projects · [05 Projects](./05-project-management.md) (LOG-405) · [17 Documents](./17-documents-file-management.md) (LOG-407) for uploaded invoices, receipts and attachments · [14 Notifications](./14-notifications-activity-center.md) (LOG-403) · [20 Automation Engine](./20-automation-engine.md) (LOG-410) for recurring expenses and monthly jobs.
>
> **Consumed by:** 05 Projects (§63 financial context and profitability), 02 CRM (customer profitability), 01 branch reporting (01 §123).

## Income & Expense Management

<!-- schema-section:start -->
> **Schema:** `LedgerRecord` ([ledger_records](../../api/prisma/schema.prisma#L7226))
<!-- schema-section:end -->

Develop a comprehensive **Income & Expense Management** module for maintaining a detailed and scalable financial record of the agency's business activity. The system should support both automatically generated financial records from uploaded invoices and fully manual financial entries, while providing a unified view of all income, expenses, VAT, payroll, recurring costs, and other financial obligations.

The module should be designed to work alongside the existing payment provider and invoicing functionality but should also operate independently for financial transactions that do not originate from the platform.

## Invoice Upload & Automatic Processing

Allow administrators to upload supplier invoices, receipts and other external financial documents into the system. Files are stored by [17 Documents](./17-documents-file-management.md); this module runs the extraction and creates the ledger record. Customer invoices issued by 08 are linked automatically and are not uploaded.

The platform should use AI/OCR to analyze uploaded invoices and automatically extract relevant information such as:

* Supplier
* Customer
* Invoice number
* Invoice date
* Due date
* Description
* Line items
* Quantities
* Unit prices
* Net value
* VAT amount
* VAT percentage
* Gross value
* Currency
* Payment information

After processing, the system should present the extracted information for user verification before creating the financial record.

Once approved, the system should automatically create the appropriate income or expense record and associate the original invoice with it.

The system should avoid creating duplicate records when the same invoice is uploaded multiple times.
